## Introduction

### What is Podcastfy Studio Hub?

Podcastfy Studio Hub is a web-based platform that transforms your written content into professional AI-generated podcast episodes. Whether you have blog posts, articles, research papers, or any text content, Podcastfy Studio Hub converts it into engaging audio conversations—no technical skills required.

### What You Can Do

- **Transform any content into audio**: Convert web articles, documents, or your own text into podcast episodes
- **Generate natural conversations**: AI creates engaging two-person dialogues from your content
- **Organize your podcast library**: Manage multiple podcast projects with unlimited episodes
- **Preview and download**: Listen to your podcasts directly in the browser and download MP3 files
- **Track progress in real-time**: Watch as your content is processed and converted to audio

### Supported Content Types

| Content Type | Description | Example |
|-------------|-------------|---------|
| **Web URLs** | Any publicly accessible webpage | Blog posts, news articles, documentation |
| **Plain Text** | Direct text input | Your own writing, research notes, scripts |

## Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

1. **Open Podcastfy Studio Hub** in your web browser
2. Click **Sign up** on the login page
3. Fill in your details:
   - **Full Name**: Your display name
   - **Email**: A valid email address (this will be your login)
   - **Password**: At least 8 characters
4. Click **Sign Up**
5. You'll be redirected to the login page with a success message
6. Enter your email and password to sign in

### Logging In

1. Navigate to the login page
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**
3. Click **Sign In**
4. You'll be taken to your Dashboard

### First-Time Dashboard

When you first log in, you'll see an empty dashboard with a message: "No projects yet." This is where all your podcast projects will appear once you create them.

## Creating Your First Podcast

Follow this step-by-step guide to create your first podcast episode:

### Step 1: Create a Project

A project is a container for your podcast episodes—think of it as your podcast show.

1. From the Dashboard, click **Create Project** (or **Create Your First Project**)
2. In the dialog that appears, enter:
   - **Project Title**: The name of your podcast (e.g., "Tech Weekly", "My Marketing Podcast")
   - **Description**: A brief description of what your podcast is about
3. Click **Create Project**
4. Your new project appears in the Dashboard

### Step 2: Create an Episode

1. Click on your project card to open it
2. Click **Create Episode** (or **Create Your First Episode**)
3. Enter an **Episode Title** (e.g., "Episode 1: Introduction to AI")
4. Click **Create Episode**
5. You'll be taken to the Episode page

### Step 3: Add Content

1. On the Episode page, click **Add Content**
2. Choose your content type:
   - **URL**: Paste a link to a web article
   - **Text**: Enter or paste your own text content
3. If using a URL:
   - Paste the full URL (including https://)
   - Click **Add Content**
4. If using Text:
   - Type or paste your content in the text box
   - Click **Add Content**
5. Your content source appears in the Content Sources list

**Tip**: You can add multiple content sources. The AI will combine them into one cohesive podcast episode.

### Step 4: Generate Your Podcast

1. Once you've added at least one content source, the **Generate Podcast** button becomes active
2. Click **Generate Podcast**
3. Watch the progress bar as your podcast is created:
   - **Extracting**: Pulling text from your content sources
   - **Generating**: AI creates the conversation script
   - **Synthesizing**: Converting the script to audio
4. When complete, the status changes to "complete"

### Step 5: Listen and Download

1. An audio player appears when generation is complete
2. Click the **play button** to preview your podcast
3. Use the **download button** or right-click → "Save audio as..." to download the MP3 file

Congratulations! You've created your first AI-generated podcast!

## Working with Episodes

Episodes are individual podcast recordings within a project.

### Viewing Episodes

1. Open a project by clicking on it
2. You'll see a list of all episodes with:
   - Episode title
   - Description
   - Generation status badge

### Creating a New Episode

1. Open your project
2. Click **Create Episode**
3. Enter the **Episode Title**
4. Click **Create Episode**
5. You'll be taken to the episode editing page

### Episode Status Badges

Each episode shows a colored status badge:

| Badge | Color | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| **draft** | Gray | Episode created but not generated |
| **queued** | Blue | Generation request submitted |
| **extracting** | Yellow | Extracting content from sources |
| **generating** | Yellow | AI is creating the transcript |
| **synthesizing** | Yellow | Converting transcript to audio |
| **complete** | Green | Podcast is ready to play/download |
| **failed** | Red | Generation encountered an error |

### Navigating Episodes

- Click on any episode card to view/edit it
- From an episode page, click **← Back to Project** to return to the project

## Adding Content Sources

Content sources are the raw materials that get transformed into your podcast.

### Types of Content Sources

#### URL Sources

Add any publicly accessible web page:
- Blog posts
- News articles
- Documentation pages
- Research articles
- Company pages

**Requirements**:
- Must be a valid URL starting with http:// or https://
- Page must be publicly accessible (no login required)
- Page must contain readable text content

#### Text Sources

Enter your own content directly:
- Original writing
- Research notes
- Interview transcripts
- Scripts or outlines
- Summaries

**Best for**:
- Content you've written yourself
- Private information not available online
- Combining ideas from multiple sources

### How to Add Content

1. Open an episode
2. Click **Add Content**
3. Select the content type:
   - Click **URL** or **Text** button at the top of the dialog
4. Enter your content:
   - For URLs: Paste the full web address
   - For Text: Type or paste your content
5. Click **Add Content**

### Managing Content Sources

Your content sources appear in a list showing:
- **Source type**: URL or Text
- **Content preview**: The URL or first 100 characters of text

**Tips for better results**:
- Add 2-3 related articles for richer content
- Include diverse perspectives on a topic
- Aim for 500-5000 words of combined source material
- More content = longer podcast episode

## Generating Podcasts

### Starting Generation

1. Add at least one content source to your episode
2. The **Generate Podcast** button activates
3. Click the button to start generation
4. Generation runs in the background—you can watch progress or navigate away

### The Generation Pipeline

Your content goes through three stages:

#### Stage 1: Extracting
- System reads your content sources
- Extracts text from URLs
- Processes and cleans the content
- Duration: Usually 5-30 seconds per source

#### Stage 2: Generating
- AI analyzes your content
- Creates a natural conversation script
- Formats dialogue between two speakers
- Duration: 30 seconds to 2 minutes depending on content length

#### Stage 3: Synthesizing
- Converts the script to spoken audio
- Uses text-to-speech technology
- Creates the final MP3 file
- Duration: 1-5 minutes depending on episode length

### Monitoring Progress

While generating, you'll see:
- A **progress bar** showing completion percentage
- The **current stage** (extracting, generating, synthesizing)
- Real-time updates as each stage completes

### What Happens if You Navigate Away?

- Generation continues in the background
- Return to the episode page to check status
- Refresh the page if the status seems stuck

## Understanding Generation Status

### Status Flow

```
draft → queued → extracting → generating → synthesizing → complete
                                                      ↘ failed
```

### Status Descriptions

| Status | What It Means | What You Can Do |
|--------|--------------|-----------------|
| **draft** | Episode created, waiting for content | Add content sources |
| **queued** | Generation requested, waiting to start | Wait for processing to begin |
| **extracting** | Reading content from your sources | Wait—extraction in progress |
| **generating** | AI creating conversation script | Wait—this may take a minute |
| **synthesizing** | Creating audio from script | Wait—almost done! |
| **complete** | Podcast is ready | Play or download your audio |
| **failed** | Something went wrong | Check content sources and retry |

### Handling Failed Generation

If your episode status shows "failed":

1. **Check your content sources**:
   - Is the URL still accessible?
   - Does the page have readable content?
   - Is there enough text to generate from?

2. **Common issues**:
   - URL behind a login or paywall
   - Website blocking automated access
   - Empty or minimal content on the page
   - Network issues during extraction

3. **To retry**:
   - Add or replace problematic content sources
   - Click **Generate Podcast** again

## Troubleshooting

### "Generate Podcast" Button is Disabled

**Problem**: The button is grayed out and won't click.

**Solution**: You need to add at least one content source before generating. Click **Add Content** and add a URL or text.

### Episode Stuck in Processing

**Problem**: The status shows "extracting," "generating," or "synthesizing" for a long time.

**Solutions**:
1. **Refresh the page** to get updated status
2. **Wait a few more minutes**—long content takes longer
3. **Check your internet connection**
4. If stuck for more than 10 minutes, the job may have failed

### Content Extraction Failed

**Problem**: URL content wasn't extracted properly.

**Solutions**:
1. **Verify the URL** is correct and accessible
2. **Try opening the URL** in a new browser tab
3. **Check if the page requires login**
4. **Use text input instead**: Copy-paste the content manually

### Audio Not Playing

**Problem**: The audio player doesn't work.

**Solutions**:
1. **Refresh the page**
2. **Try a different browser** (Chrome, Firefox, Safari)
3. **Check your browser's audio permissions**
4. **Download the file** and play it locally

### Login Issues

**Problem**: Can't log in to your account.

**Solutions**:
1. **Check your email** is spelled correctly
2. **Verify your password** (passwords are case-sensitive)
3. **Clear browser cache** and try again
4. **Try a different browser**

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: Is my content private?**
A: Yes, your projects and episodes are only visible to you. Each user has their own isolated workspace.

**Q: How long can my podcasts be?**
A: Episode length depends on your source content. Typically:
- 500 words → 2-3 minute podcast
- 2000 words → 5-10 minute podcast
- 5000+ words → 15-30 minute podcast

**Q: What audio format are the podcasts?**
A: All podcasts are generated as MP3 files, compatible with all major platforms and devices.

**Q: How many projects/episodes can I create?**
A: There's no limit on the number of projects or episodes you can create.

### Content Questions

**Q: What types of URLs work best?**
A: Blog posts, articles, and documentation pages work best. Avoid:
- Pages requiring login
- Video-heavy pages without text
- PDF links (direct PDF support coming soon)
- Social media posts

**Q: Can I use paywalled content?**
A: No, the system cannot access content behind paywalls. Use the Text input option to paste content you have access to.

**Q: How much content should I add?**
A: For best results:
- Minimum: 300 words
- Ideal: 1000-3000 words
- Maximum: 10,000 words per episode

**Q: Can I mix URLs and text?**
A: Yes! You can add multiple content sources of different types. They'll all be combined into one episode.

### Technical Questions

**Q: Why did my generation fail?**
A: Common reasons include:
- URL couldn't be accessed
- Not enough content to generate from
- Temporary service issues
Try again with different content or use the Text input option.

**Q: How long does generation take?**
A: Typically 1-5 minutes depending on content length and current system load.

**Q: Can I edit the generated audio?**
A: Currently, you can download the audio and edit it in audio editing software. Direct editing features are planned for future releases.

**Q: What voices are used?**
A: The system uses high-quality AI voices. Voice customization options are planned for future releases.

## Tips for Great Podcasts

### Content Selection

1. **Choose focused content**: Articles on a single topic work better than broad overviews
2. **Pick quality sources**: Well-written articles produce better conversations
3. **Combine perspectives**: Add 2-3 articles on the same topic for richer discussions
4. **Check readability**: Avoid highly technical content with lots of jargon

### Organization

1. **Use descriptive names**: "Episode 12 - Marketing Strategy" vs "Episode 12"
2. **Group related episodes**: Use projects to organize series or themes
3. **Plan your series**: Create episodes in the order you want them

### Best Practices

1. **Preview before publishing**: Always listen to the full episode before sharing
2. **Start small**: Try a short article first to understand the process
3. **Iterate**: If results aren't perfect, try different source content
4. **Add context**: Include introductory text with your URLs for better framing
